Cavendish Space

Places built to fit bodyminds, not the other way round.

Ways in

  • Show how you feel Cavendish Cards Point at a picture instead of explaining. No reading needed, no scoring, and nothing is shared until you turn it up.
  • Set up a room Zone a room Work out which zones a room can already hold, what would change that, and print signs for the door.
  • Read the idea What is a Cavendish Space? Caves, campfires, watering holes, and the habitat that holds them. Who Cavendish was, what a space is made of, and why it is built this way.

Also here: the guidebook, the facilitator sheet, example spreads, what to do with a pile of spreads after an event, the livable worlds checklist, and how this fits ARLES. Everything is free and CC0.
